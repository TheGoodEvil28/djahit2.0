About Us

Djahit is a SaaS platform that connects users with local tailors to repair and modify clothing. With the fashion industry producing 92 million tons of textile waste annually, Djahit makes online ordering easy while supporting sustainability and small businesses.

Our Mission

Sustainability in Fashion

The fashion industry contributes 10% of global carbon emissions. Clothing repair can reduce textile footprint by up to 30%.

Empowering Local Tailors

The platform helps local tailors find customers and establish sustainable income sources.

Reducing Textile Waste

Clothing repair reduces tons of textile waste that end up in landfills each year.
